# Approved v1.0.0 rights declaration

On 10 October 2026 the authors approved CC BY 4.0 for the existing project-authored public ConstraintArb reproducibility package: benchmark and analysis source, synthetic scenarios, saved factual scoring inputs/observations, frozen summaries, preregistrations, authored documentation and generated figures. Attribution remains Taewan Kim followed by Taehoon Kim.

LICENSE links the complete CC BY 4.0 legal terms. The license permits sharing and adaptation, including commercial use, subject to its attribution and other terms. Third-party dependencies are installed separately under their respective licenses. No private IEEE manuscript assets, portraits, downloaded literature, raw model-response/rationale payloads or external proprietary assets are added by this grant.

The historical `data/export_provenance.json` field `license` records the earlier unlicensed export state. It is deliberately preserved as frozen provenance. The current v1.0.0 rights grant in LICENSE, CITATION.cff and this declaration supersedes that earlier state; no scientific observations or frozen hashes were changed.
