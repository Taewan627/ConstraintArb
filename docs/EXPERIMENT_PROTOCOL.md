# Frozen experimental protocol

Stage 1 measures explicit-cost arbitration; its recognition collection failed and was not treated as scientific recognition evidence. Stage 2 compares graph-only and explicit costs with a separate recognition probe. Stage 3a examines the original dependent-directed cue in the affected v4-pro model. Stage 3b adds other models and a generic-deliberation control. Stage 3c examines restatement and alternative cue wording in v4-pro and v4.1-flash.

Stages were registered prospectively before their own data collection, with sequential adaptations disclosed. Original preregistrations are provided; they are not a claim that all stages were globally fixed before Stage 1. Configurations and seeds are retained unchanged. Trial exclusions and scoring flags are retained unchanged in public saved tables.
